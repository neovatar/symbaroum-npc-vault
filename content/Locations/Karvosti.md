# Karvosti

## Description
A weathered trading town on the last road before the true forest begins.
Ambrian officials rub shoulders with Ravenfolk traders here, and the
Queen's Rangers keep an official (if thin) presence.

## Notable NPCs
- [[Elyn Thornwood]] — Queen's Rangers scout, works out of Karvosti

## Controlling faction
[[Queen's Rangers]] (nominally — the town largely governs itself)
