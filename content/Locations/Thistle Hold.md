# Thistle Hold

## Description
A hastily-fortified logging outpost cut into the edge of Davokar, thrown up
in the years after Ambria's expansion east. Wood-smoke haze, mud streets,
and the constant sound of axes — half boomtown, half garrison.

## Notable NPCs
- [[Kastor Rovin]] — Iron Pact fixer running goods through the hold
- [[Old Maren]] — herbalist, keeps to the edge of town

## Controlling faction
[[Iron Pact]] (in practice, if not on paper)
